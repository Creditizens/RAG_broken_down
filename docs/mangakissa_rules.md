# Mangakissa Rules
## Level 5 · Shibuya, Tokyo · English Guest Guide

Welcome to our manga and internet café on Level 5 in Shibuya. Please help keep this shared space quiet, clean, comfortable, and safe. Manga heroes may cause chaos; guests should not.

This is a fictional company-policy document for a RAG lab, set at a mangakissa on Level 5 in Shibuya. Its policies and characters are illustrative, not verified policies of an actual business or statements of Japanese law. No prices or opening hours are specified; consult the reception desk for the current information in this scenario.


<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-001 | Entry | Check in before settling in
All guests must check in at reception before entering the booth or reading areas. An empty chair is not a reservation, even if it appears to be calling your name.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-002 | Entry | Confirm the correct floor
Our café is on Level 5. Check the building directory and café sign before entering. A dramatic entrance into the wrong office does not count as check-in.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-003 | Entry | Provide required identification privately
If reception requests identification for registration or a particular service, present it directly to staff. Ask which documents are accepted before purchasing your session. Never leave identity documents on a shared desk.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-004 | Entry | Ask about age restrictions
Guests visiting with children or teenagers must ask reception about applicable age and time restrictions before paying. Staff will explain which areas and services are available.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-005 | Entry | Accept the assigned seat
Use the booth or seat assigned at check-in. Ask reception before changing seats, even if the neighboring chair looks like the throne of the Manga Kingdom.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-006 | Entry | Explain accessibility needs
Tell reception if you need an accessible route, suitable seating, or assistance understanding instructions. Staff should explain available options before a session is purchased.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-007 | Payment | Confirm the rate before starting
Before accepting a seat, confirm your selected plan, its duration, included services, and any extra charges. The displayed reception price list governs charges; this guide does not establish a price.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-008 | Payment | Know when the clock starts
Session time begins at the start time recorded during check-in. Check that time before leaving reception. Reading the welcome sign very slowly does not pause the clock.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-009 | Payment | Check the payment method
Ask reception which payment methods are accepted before starting a session. Do not assume a foreign bank card, mobile wallet, or heroic smile will work.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-010 | Payment | Pay when reception requests
Follow the payment timing explained at check-in, including any required advance payment. Guests must settle all outstanding charges before leaving after checkout.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-011 | Payment | Track your own end time
Guests are responsible for monitoring their session end time. Set a silent alarm if needed. Mr W finishing one more chapter does not create one more free hour.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-012 | Payment | Overtime is chargeable
Remaining beyond the purchased session may incur the overtime charges displayed at reception. Ask staff how overtime is calculated before the session begins; do not assume a grace period exists.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-013 | Payment | Request extensions early
Ask reception for an extension before your current session expires. Extensions depend on availability and the applicable rate. Silence from the front desk is not approval.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-014 | Payment | Package changes need approval
Ask reception whether your plan can be changed before expecting a different rate. Longer-stay packages are not automatically applied retroactively unless staff explicitly confirm that adjustment.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-015 | Payment | Extras are separate unless included
Printing, showers, rentals, food, and other optional services may carry additional fees when offered. Confirm the fee before using a service. A free-drink plan does not mean a free-everything plan.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-016 | Payment | Ask before leaving temporarily
Before stepping outside during a session, ask reception about re-entry and any required pass. Session time continues during temporary exits unless staff expressly approve a different arrangement.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-017 | Payment | Raise billing questions calmly
If a charge looks incorrect, bring your session slip or receipt to reception and explain the issue. Staff should review the recorded times and services with you. Volume does not improve arithmetic.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-018 | Payment | Early departures and refunds
Ask reception about cancellation and early-departure terms before paying. Leaving early does not automatically qualify for a refund; any refund must follow the terms explained for your purchase.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-019 | Quiet | Keep conversations brief and soft
Use a low voice throughout the reading and booth areas. Hold longer conversations only in a location approved by staff. Your plot theory can be excellent and still be too loud.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-020 | Quiet | Silence your phone
Set phones and other devices to silent mode before entering the seating areas. Disable keyboard clicks and repeated notification sounds. Yellow Hat's group chat is not the café soundtrack.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-021 | Quiet | Take calls in an approved location
Do not make or answer voice or video calls in reading areas or booths. Ask staff where calls are permitted. Whispering into a speakerphone is still a speakerphone call.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-022 | Quiet | Use headphones without sound leakage
Headphones are required for audible media. Keep the volume low enough that neighboring guests cannot hear it. If Junko can identify your anime opening from three booths away, lower it.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-023 | Quiet | Keep gaming reactions quiet
Gaming is welcome where the assigned equipment supports it, but shouting, desk slamming, and loud voice chat are prohibited. Defeat the boss without becoming the noise complaint.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-024 | Quiet | Use silent alarms
Use vibration or another silent reminder for checkout. Do not set a ringing alarm in a shared area. Your nap's final boss must not awaken the entire floor.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-025 | Quiet | Report persistent sleep noise discreetly
If snoring or another sleep-related sound repeatedly disturbs you, tell reception quietly. Do not wake, film, or mock another guest. Staff may discreetly offer a seat change or speak with the guest.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-026 | Quiet | Handle doors and furniture gently
Close booth doors softly and avoid scraping chairs or dropping bags. The building does not require sound effects every time someone sits down.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-027 | Manga | Take a small reading stack
Keep no more than five café volumes at your seat at one time. Return finished books before collecting more so other guests can enjoy the series too.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-028 | Manga | Return books to the designated point
Place finished volumes on the labeled return cart. If you cannot locate it, ask staff. Do not guess a shelf location and send Volume 8 on a mysterious side quest.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-029 | Manga | Keep books inside the café
Café manga and magazines must remain inside the premises. They are for on-site reading, not souvenirs. A cliffhanger is not permission to take the book home.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-030 | Manga | Protect pages and bindings
Do not write in books, fold page corners, remove pages, or force bindings flat. Use a clean removable bookmark. Mr W's review belongs in conversation, not in permanent marker on page 42.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-031 | Manga | Handle books with clean hands
Clean and dry your hands before handling shared books. Keep food, sauces, and open drinks away from pages. Curry is a meal, not a manga restoration technique.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-032 | Manga | Report damaged books
Tell staff if you find or accidentally cause torn, wet, stained, or loose pages. Do not attempt repairs with tape or glue. Prompt reporting helps protect the collection.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-033 | Drink Area | Use your own clean cup
Use the cups provided for guests and follow the dispenser instructions. Do not drink directly from a dispenser or place a used cup against its nozzle.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-034 | Drink Area | Included drinks are for registered guests
If drinks are included in your plan, they are for your own consumption during your session. Do not supply drinks to unregistered visitors or fill containers for people outside the café.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-035 | Drink Area | Do not fill personal bottles
Do not fill personal bottles, thermoses, or takeaway containers at the drink dispensers. Use the provided cups. Yellow Hat may be thirsty, but this is not a beverage export terminal.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-036 | Drink Area | Clean small spills and report larger ones
Wipe up small counter spills with the provided supplies. Tell staff immediately about floor spills, hot-liquid spills, or any spill you cannot safely clean. Do not leave a slippery surprise for the next guest.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-037 | Drink Area | Sort cups and rubbish
Put used cups and rubbish in the labeled collection points. Empty leftover liquids only into the designated liquid-disposal point. Ask staff if a label is unclear.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-038 | Drink Area | Keep the queue moving
Wait your turn at dispensers and step aside once your drink is ready. Choose your next flavor away from the machine. Junko should not need a reservation to reach the hot water.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-039 | Drink Area | Report dispenser problems
If a machine leaks, jams, runs empty, or displays an error, notify staff. Do not open panels, strike the machine, or attempt repairs. Coffee responds poorly to intimidation.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-040 | Food | Ask before bringing outside food
Get reception approval before consuming outside food. Staff may restrict strong-smelling, messy, or unsuitable items and direct you to an appropriate eating area.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-041 | Food | Keep meals away from equipment
Eat only where staff allow it and keep food away from keyboards, sockets, and shared equipment. Put drinks on a stable surface away from books and electronics.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-042 | Cleanliness | Leave your seat tidy
Before checkout, collect your belongings, remove rubbish, and return borrowed items to their designated points. Your booth should not resemble the aftermath of a twelve-volume battle.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-043 | Cleanliness | Keep restrooms clean
Use restroom facilities as labeled, place rubbish in the appropriate bins, and report blockages or missing supplies. Do not wash dishes, clothing, or shoes in restroom sinks.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-044 | Cleanliness | Follow shower instructions when available
If a shower service is offered, reserve and pay as reception directs. Observe the assigned time, use only supplied or approved products, and place used towels in the labeled collection point.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-045 | Cleanliness | Follow footwear signs
Keep footwear on unless the assigned seating area is explicitly marked for shoe removal. In those areas, place shoes in the designated spot and keep them out of the aisle.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-046 | Cleanliness | Avoid strong scents
Avoid spraying perfume, deodorant, or other scented products in shared areas. If a hygiene or odor concern affects your visit, speak privately to staff rather than commenting on another guest.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-047 | Safety | Keep aisles and exits clear
Store bags within your assigned space or in a staff-approved storage location. Never block doors, stairs, fire equipment, or evacuation routes. Your suitcase does not get its own seat in the aisle.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-048 | Safety | Protect your valuables
Keep wallets, passports, phones, and other valuables with you or in a provided locker used according to its instructions. Do not ask an unfamiliar guest to supervise your belongings.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-049 | Safety | Respect booth boundaries
Do not enter, reach into, or look over another guest's booth. Knock only when staff direct you to do so. Curiosity about someone else's manga selection is not an access pass.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-050 | Safety | Report concerning conduct, not appearances
Report observable behavior such as trying booth handles, following guests, or handling unattended belongings. Clothing, nationality, language, and appearance alone are not reasons to suspect someone. Yellow Hat wearing a yellow hat is simply a wardrobe decision.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-051 | Safety | Do not patrol or confront people
If someone repeatedly circulates near booths and their actions concern you, tell reception where and what you observed. Do not follow, corner, search, or accuse the person. Staff handle access checks.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-052 | Safety | Report harassment promptly
Unwanted touching, threats, repeated unwanted conversation, and discriminatory abuse are prohibited. Move toward reception if safe and tell staff what happened. Guests may ask for help without first confronting the person involved.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-053 | Safety | Leave unattended items alone
Do not open, move, or inspect an unfamiliar unattended bag. Tell staff its location and follow their instructions. This is not the opening scene of your detective career.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-054 | Safety | Hand found property to staff
Bring ordinary found items, such as a loose key or forgotten book, to reception when safe to do so. For an unattended bag or an item that appears hazardous, leave it in place and alert staff instead.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-055 | Safety | Keep staff-only areas private
Do not enter storage rooms, the reception workspace, maintenance areas, or other spaces marked staff only. Junko's break room is not a hidden bonus level.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-056 | Safety | Follow emergency directions
On hearing an alarm or receiving evacuation instructions, stop your activity and follow staff and posted directions to the designated exit route. Leave bulky belongings behind and do not use the lift during a fire evacuation.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-057 | Safety | No smoking or vaping inside
Smoking and vaping are prohibited throughout this café, including booths and restrooms. Ask reception whether the building provides an approved smoking location elsewhere.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-058 | Safety | No disruptive intoxication
Do not consume alcohol inside the café. Guests whose intoxication causes disruption or unsafe behavior may be refused entry or asked to leave. A quiet manga session requires a guest who can follow instructions.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-059 | Privacy | Ask before taking photos
Ask staff before photographing the café interior. Do not photograph, film, or record other guests, their screens, receipts, or belongings without their explicit permission. A blurred background can still contain a person who wanted privacy.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-060 | Computers | Sign out before checkout
Sign out of all accounts used on shared computers and remove your downloaded personal files before leaving. Do not assume closing a browser signs you out. Ask staff for help if needed.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-061 | Computers | Do not change system settings
Do not install software, bypass access controls, disconnect café equipment, or change system settings without staff approval. Report technical problems instead of attempting repairs.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-062 | Computers | Keep shared screens appropriate
Do not display graphic sexual or violent material where other guests can see it. Follow posted restrictions for age-limited materials and ask staff if unsure where a particular item may be viewed.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-063 | Equipment | Report damage immediately
Tell staff immediately if equipment breaks or liquid spills on it. Stop using affected equipment and do not touch wet plugs or sockets. Staff will assess the situation and explain any applicable charges before requesting payment.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-064 | Courtesy | Respect staff and other guests
Follow reasonable staff directions and speak respectfully to everyone. Mr W, Yellow Hat, Junko, first-time tourists, and regular readers follow the same house rules. Familiarity with the café does not unlock immunity.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-065 | Courtesy | Ask when a sign is unclear
If an English translation or pictogram is unclear, ask reception before proceeding. Staff can point out the relevant area or explain the rule. A polite question is better than an accidental side quest.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-066 | Checkout | Complete checkout at reception
Return your session slip, access card, and rented items as directed, then complete checkout at reception. Leaving your booth does not end your session; the checkout time recorded by reception does.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-067 | Enforcement | Cooperate with rule reminders
Staff may ask guests to correct a rule violation, change seats, or leave for serious or repeated misconduct. Applicable charges remain subject to the purchase terms; staff should explain the reason for any action and any billing consequences.

<<<MANGAKISSA_RULE>>>

### MK-068 | Departure | Keep the building quiet too
Speak softly while leaving through shared corridors, lifts, and stairs, and avoid gathering in front of neighboring businesses. Please carry the peaceful café atmosphere downstairs with you. The cliffhanger may travel; the shouting should not.
